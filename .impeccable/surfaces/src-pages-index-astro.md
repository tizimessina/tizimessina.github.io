# Homepage (single page, EN at / and ES-AR at /es/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters and employers (plus peers) on a laptop. Action: see the projects; contact is always one click away. Proof: real AgroApp and homelab screenshots, agroapp.dev, the two repos, and concrete homelab facts. No experience section; education stands in for it. Static Astro build for GitHub Pages. The user rejected the round-1 manual-page world as gimmicky, plain, dense, serif/cream/amber: do not return to any of that.

Memorable moment: the headline's two inline app icons (AgroApp sprout, homelab server rack) and the dark showcase that shows both real projects at once, each a door into its section.

Confirmed 2026-09-27: the hero states "Open to my first junior role" and the contact band closes on it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Tiziano presented like a well-made product launch: a confident first-person headline, real project screenshots as the hero imagery, and short scannable rows. It refuses the manual/gimmick concept, long text blocks, identical-card grids, and neon hacker styling.

OWN-WORLD: Light, cool near-white page (#F5F5F4 family) with near-black ink. Dark stage bands (#0A0A0B) wherever screenshots appear, so they glow like product shots. One strong electric blue (#3B5BFF) for actions, links and the active tab only. Geist Variable carries everything, with a tight, large display. Geist Mono only for small technical labels and stack tags. Screenshots sit in a softly rounded browser-like frame with a real offset shadow.

STORY: Visitors understand that Tiziano (4th-year Information Systems Engineering student, UTN Rosario) builds software and runs it on a self-hosted homelab. They believe it because they see the real screens and specific facts, then open a project or make contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim sticky nav (name mark left; Projects, Skills, Education links; EN/ES switch; blue Contact button). A large first-person headline across roughly 60% of the width, with a one-line identity sub-line, and two buttons: See projects (primary blue) and Contact me. At the bottom, the dark stage rises into view with its AgroApp / Homelab tabs and the AgroApp screenshot partially visible above the fold at 1280×800.

FORM: Launch Page, 1st on the ordered list (Impeccable's pick, chosen over the roll); seed 127a8396. Signature interaction: the two-project showcase (frames lift on hover and link to each project), plus restrained scroll-in reveals on the project articles (reduced-motion safe). The tabbed crossfade was retired after critique because it hid the homelab.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
